## The fine print that changes the math

**The codes don't stack.** JustHost states outright that they can't be combined with other discounts. If a long billing cycle already carries its own discount, run both paths to the cart total and pick the lower one.

**Both work on custom configurations.** Standard plans and custom-built VPS configurations are covered, and existing customers can use them when ordering a new service. It's new services only; the codes won't retroactively discount a server you already own.

**"Unlimited traffic" means a shared port, not a private pipe.** The 300, 400, 500 and 750 Mbps labels are fair-share port speeds. JustHost publishes theoretical monthly transfer against each: 300 Mbps works out to 93TB+, 400 Mbps to 124TB+, 500 Mbps to 155TB+, and 750 Mbps to 232TB+. Those are arithmetic ceilings at full port speed, not throughput you can hold around the clock, and load differs between data centres.

**No mainland China optimisation.** JustHost runs pure international routing. If your visitors are in mainland China, the Hong Kong, Tokyo, Singapore and Los Angeles locations are the ones to test, and testing matters more than distance suggests. Looking Glass on the site lets you check latency and routing per location before you buy.

**Changing data centre and IP is self-service.** You can move a server between locations from the control panel and each VPS gets up to 50 free IP changes. Migration can interrupt running services, so take a backup first.

**Payment and refunds.** Cards, PayPal, Alipay, UnionPay, PerfectMoney and assorted cryptocurrencies are all accepted. Third-party reviews mention a 15-day money-back guarantee, but that isn't confirmed on JustHost's own policy page, so read the current terms before you rely on it.

**Location count depends on who's counting.** A late-October review puts coverage at 41 countries and 80 locations; an August review says about 50 data centres. Both could be right under different definitions. Locations named consistently across sources include Hong Kong, Tokyo, Singapore, Los Angeles, Seattle, New York, Chicago, Dallas, Atlanta, Toronto, Frankfurt, Amsterdam, London, Paris, Zurich, Helsinki, Stockholm, Warsaw, Milan, Palermo, Vienna, Prague, Riga, Vilnius, Bucharest, Sofia, Madrid and Dublin. Stock varies by location at any given moment.

JustHost sells unmanaged KVM VPS on international routing, and the price reflects that.
